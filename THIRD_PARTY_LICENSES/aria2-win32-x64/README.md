# aria2 Windows x64 component notices

These notices apply only to the Windows x64 aria2 engine `1.37.0-motrix.16` distributed by Motrix, from [`motrixapp/aria2` commit `e093973f113f0a880f9757a2ad0258cb2356295e`](https://github.com/motrixapp/aria2/tree/e093973f113f0a880f9757a2ad0258cb2356295e). They describe the selected components below; they do not describe the dependencies of other platform builds.

The [release](https://github.com/motrixapp/aria2/releases/tag/v1.37.0-motrix.16) identifies the Windows archive `aria2c-1.37.0-motrix.16-win32-x64.zip`. [components.json](components.json) records its archive and executable SHA-256 values, exact source commits, license-file hashes, source-archive URLs and hashes, and the libssh2 patches.

The libssh2 upstream version is 1.11.1. This engine applies three patches from the pinned aria2 commit: [CVE-2026-55199](https://github.com/motrixapp/aria2/blob/e093973f113f0a880f9757a2ad0258cb2356295e/patches/libssh2-1.11.1-cve-2026-55199.patch), [CVE-2026-55200](https://github.com/motrixapp/aria2/blob/e093973f113f0a880f9757a2ad0258cb2356295e/patches/libssh2-1.11.1-cve-2026-55200.patch), and [security rollup](https://github.com/motrixapp/aria2/blob/e093973f113f0a880f9757a2ad0258cb2356295e/patches/libssh2-1.11.1-security-rollup-2026.patch). Their paths and SHA-256 values are recorded separately in `components.json`. The wslay version comes from its [vendored configure.ac](https://github.com/motrixapp/aria2/blob/e093973f113f0a880f9757a2ad0258cb2356295e/deps/wslay/configure.ac#L24); its exact contents are identified by the aria2 commit.

The [Windows build recipe](https://github.com/motrixapp/aria2/blob/e093973f113f0a880f9757a2ad0258cb2356295e/Dockerfile.mingw), [configuration](https://github.com/motrixapp/aria2/blob/e093973f113f0a880f9757a2ad0258cb2356295e/mingw-config), [dependency pins](https://github.com/motrixapp/aria2/blob/e093973f113f0a880f9757a2ad0258cb2356295e/scripts/ci/deps.env), and [release build job](https://github.com/motrixapp/aria2/actions/runs/35833737683/job/107092178635) provide the build provenance for this list. Source-archive hashes identify recipe inputs; the license texts here were retrieved from the exact official Git sources, not independently compared against the contents of those archives.
